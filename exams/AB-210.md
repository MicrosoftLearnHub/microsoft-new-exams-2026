## 🧠 5 Scenario-Based Practice Questions with In-Depth Explanations

### Question 01
**Scenario / Problem Statement:**  
Which purpose-built Dynamics 365 Sales AI agent autonomously researches prospect companies, identifies recent news and executive changes, and enriches lead records prior to sales outreach?

- **A)** Sales Research Agent
- **B)** Finance Ledger Agent
- **C)** Warehouse Packing Agent
- **D)** Printer Driver Agent

- **Correct Answer:** `A`
- **Detailed Architectural Explanation:** The Sales Research Agent runs background enrichment across corporate sources and web intelligence to provide sellers with rich context on target accounts.

---

### Question 02
**Scenario / Problem Statement:**  
How does the Sales Qualification Agent in Dynamics 365 assist sales development representatives (SDRs)?

- **A)** It terminates sales reps automatically
- **B)** It engages inbound leads via email and chat, answers product inquiries, evaluates BANT criteria, and schedules introductory meetings
- **C)** It prints invoices to paper
- **D)** It reformats hard drives

- **Correct Answer:** `B`
- **Detailed Architectural Explanation:** The Sales Qualification Agent acts autonomously to nurture leads, answer initial questions, assess qualification scores, and book meetings for account executives.

---

### Question 03
**Scenario / Problem Statement:**  
Which feature in Dynamics 365 Sales uses predictive machine learning to assign a likelihood-to-convert score (1-100) to incoming prospect records?

- **A)** Predictive Lead Scoring
- **B)** Manual Excel sorting
- **C)** Random number generator
- **D)** Static workflow wait condition

- **Correct Answer:** `A`
- **Detailed Architectural Explanation:** Predictive Lead Scoring evaluates historical conversion patterns and attributes (lead source, industry, engagement) to prioritize leads for sales reps.

---

### Question 04
**Scenario / Problem Statement:**  
When a sales representative opens an Opportunity record in Dynamics 365, how does Copilot in Sales boost immediate productivity?

- **A)** By playing video games
- **B)** By generating an instant AI Opportunity Summary highlighting key contacts, budget status, recent emails, and pending action items
- **C)** By deleting old emails
- **D)** By disconnecting from the network

- **Correct Answer:** `B`
- **Detailed Architectural Explanation:** Copilot generates a comprehensive Opportunity Summary synthesizing timeline interactions, stakeholder sentiment, and recommended next steps.

---

### Question 05
**Scenario / Problem Statement:**  
How is seller collaboration enhanced when integrating Dynamics 365 Sales with Microsoft Teams?

- **A)** Sales deal rooms with linked Dynamics records, automated deal updates, and embedded Copilot meeting recaps with action item extraction
- **B)** Disabling chat for all sales employees
- **C)** Forcing employees to use analog phone lines
- **D)** Blocking external emails

- **Correct Answer:** `A`
- **Detailed Architectural Explanation:** Teams deal rooms and embedded Copilot meeting summarization allow cross-functional sales teams to collaborate seamlessly with real-time CRM synchronization.
